### Lent and Holy Week

#### [25] First Sunday in Lent (Invocavit)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 76):** *Am ersten Sontag in der Fasten/wie Christus in der Wüsten vom Teuffel versucht wirt. Ma[tth]. 4. [partly blotted]* [On the First Sunday in Lent: how Christ is tempted by the devil in the wilderness. Matthew 4.]

**German (PDF p. 79)**

> HERR Gott himlischer Vater/wir bitten dich/weil der arge feind/für vnd für vns nachschleichet/vnnd wie ein brüllender Löwe vmbher gehet/vnd suchet/ob er vns köndte fressen/Du wöllest vmb deines Sones Christi Jesu willen/mit dem heyligen Geyst zu vns setzen/vnnd durch dein wort vnsere hertzen stercken/auff das der feinde vns nicht obsiege/vnnd wir in deiner gnade für vnnd für bleyben/vnd endlich selig werden/Amen.

**Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, inasmuch as the evil enemy doth continually creep after us, and as a roaring lion doth walk about, seeking to devour us: We beseech Thee for the sake of Thy Son, Jesus Christ, to stand by us with the Holy Ghost, and to strengthen our hearts by Thy word, that our enemy may not prevail over us, but that we may evermore abide in Thy grace, and at last be saved; *through the same, Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ, our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**
- "the adversary doth continually afflict us" → "the evil enemy doth continually creep after us" (*der arge feind/für vnd für vns nachschleichet*).
- "for the sake of the suffering and death of Thy Son" → "for the sake of Thy Son" (*vmb deines Sones Christi Jesu willen*; "the suffering and death" is not in the German).
- "to help us by the grace of the Holy Spirit" → "to stand by us with the Holy Ghost" (*mit dem heyligen Geyst zu vns setzen*, literally "set [Thyself] by us").
- "and be preserved unto everlasting life" → "and at last be saved" (*vnd endlich selig werden*).

#### [26] Second Sunday in Lent (Reminiscere)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 80):** *Am anderen Sontag inn der Fasten/vom dem [sic, for von dem] Heydnischen Frewlin/des Dochter besessen war/Matthei am 15.* [On the Second Sunday in Lent: of the heathen woman whose daughter was possessed. Matthew 15.]

**German (PDF p. 82)**

> HErr Got himlischer Vatter/wir biten dich/du wöllest durch deinen heyligen Geyst/den glauben vnnd hoffnung auff dein gnad vnnd barmhertzigkeyt in vnserm hertzen fest vñ gewiß machen/auff das/ob wir gleich vnsers gewissens/sünde/vnd vnwirdigkeyt halb vrsach haben vnns zu förchten/wir dennoch an deiner genade/wie das Cananeisch Weyblin/fest hangen bleyben/vnd durch Christum hilff vnnd rettung in allerley anfechtung vnd nöten bey dir finden/Amen.

**Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, we beseech Thee, by Thy Holy Ghost make faith and hope in Thy grace and mercy firm and sure in our hearts, so that, although we have reason to fear because of our conscience, our sin, and our unworthiness, we may nevertheless, like the woman of Canaan, hold fast to Thy grace, and in all manner of temptation and need find help and deliverance with Thee through Christ, *who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**

- "and in every trial and temptation find Thee a very present help and refuge" → "and in all manner of temptation and need find help and deliverance with Thee through Christ" (*vnd durch Christum hilff vnnd rettung in allerley anfechtung vnd nöten bey dir finden*).
- TLHy's "through Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ our Lord," gives way to Dietrich's "through Christ," which the conclusion follows (§2.4c).

#### [27] Third Sunday in Lent (Oculi)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 83):** *Am dritten Sontag/wie Jesus einen Teuffel außtreibet/der stum̃ war. Luce am 11.* [On the Third Sunday: how Jesus casts out a devil that was dumb. Luke 11.]

**German (PDF p. 85)**

> HErr Gott himlischer Vatter/der du deinen Sun vnsern Herren Christum Jesum darumb hast lassen mensch werden/das er des Teuffels Tyranney wehren/vñ vns menschen wider den argen feind/schützen soll/wir dancken dir erstlich für solche gnedige hilff/vnd bitten darnach du wöllest in allen anfechtungen genedig zu vns setzen/für sicherheyt vns behüten/vñ in deim wort vñ deiner forcht/durch deinen heyligen Geyst genedig erhalten/auff das wir biß ans ende für dem bösen feinde befridet bleyben/vnd durch deinen Sun ewig selig werden/AMEN.

**Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, who for this cause didst suffer Thy Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, to become man, that He should restrain the tyranny of the devil, and defend us men against the evil enemy: We give thanks unto Thee first for such gracious help, and then we beseech Thee graciously to stand by us in all temptations, to preserve us from carnal security, and by Thy Holy Ghost graciously to keep us in Thy word and Thy fear, that unto the end we may remain safe from the evil enemy, and obtain eternal salvation through Thy Son, *who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**
- "who hast sent Thy Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, to take upon Himself our flesh" → "who for this cause didst suffer Thy Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, to become man" (*darumb hast lassen mensch werden*).
- "that He might overcome the devil" → "that He should restrain the tyranny of the devil" (*des Teuffels Tyranney wehren*), as at [3].
- "and defend us poor sinners against the adversary" → "and defend us men against the evil enemy" (*vns menschen wider den argen feind/schützen*).
- "We give thanks unto Thee for Thy merciful help" → "We give thanks unto Thee first for such gracious help" (*erstlich für solche gnedige hilff*).
- "and we beseech Thee to attend us with Thy grace in all temptations" → "and then we beseech Thee graciously to stand by us in all temptations" (*vnd bitten darnach du wöllest in allen anfechtungen genedig zu vns setzen*), as at [25].
- "to keep us" → "graciously to keep us" (*genedig erhalten*).
- "that unto the end we may be delivered from the enemy" → "that unto the end we may remain safe from the evil enemy" (*biß ans ende für dem bösen feinde befridet bleyben*).
- "and obtain eternal salvation, through the same, Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ our Lord," → "and obtain eternal salvation through Thy Son," which is Dietrich's own close (*vnd durch deinen Sun ewig selig werden*); TLHy's conclusion continues it (§2.4d).
- "Holy Ghost" for "Holy Spirit."

#### [28] Fourth Sunday in Lent (Laetare)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 86):** *Am Sontag zu Mitfastē/wie der Herr Christus fünfftausent Man/weyb vnnd kind nicht mit gerechnet/mit fünff brodten vnd zwen fischen speyset/vnd zwölff körb vberblieben/Johan. 6.* [On the Sunday of Mid-Lent: how the Lord Christ feeds five thousand men, not counting women and children, with five loaves and two fishes, and twelve baskets were left over. John 6.]

**German (PDF p. 88)**

> HErr Gott himlischer Vatter/der du durch deinen Sun in der wüsten fünff tausent Mañ/weyb vnnd kind nit mitgerechnet/nur mit fünff brodt vñ zwen fischen/reychlich/gespeyset hast/Wir bitten dich/sey mit deinem segen auch gnedig bey vns/behüte für dem geytz vnd sorgen der narung/das wir dein Reych vnd dein gerechtigkeyt am ersten süchen/vnd deine milte Väterliche güte in allem/was wir zu leyb vnd seel bedürffen/gnedigklich spüren durch Jesum Christum vnsern Herren/AMEN.

**Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, who by Thy Son didst richly feed in the desert five thousand men, besides women and children, with but five loaves and two fishes: We beseech Thee to abide graciously also with us with Thy blessing. Preserve us from avarice and the cares of this life, that we may seek first Thy kingdom and Thy righteousness, and graciously perceive Thy bountiful fatherly goodness in all that we need for body and soul; through Jesus Christ our Lord, *who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**
- "didst feed five thousand men in the desert with five loaves and two fishes" → "didst richly feed in the desert five thousand men, besides women and children, with but five loaves and two fishes" (*fünff tausent Mañ/weyb vnnd kind nit mitgerechnet/nur mit fünff brodt vñ zwen fischen/reychlich/gespeyset*; "besides women and children" is the King James wording of Matthew 14:21).

- "and in all things perceive Thy fatherly goodness" → "and graciously perceive Thy bountiful fatherly goodness in all that we need for body and soul" (*deine milte Väterliche güte in allem/was wir zu leyb vnd seel bedürffen/gnedigklich spüren*).
- "through Jesus Christ" → "through Jesus Christ our Lord," which is Dietrich's own close (*durch Jesum Christum vnsern Herren*); TLHy's conclusion continues it (§2.4d).
- The OLM web text omits the comma before "world without end"; restored.

**Note.** "The cares of this life" is TLHy's King James wording (Luke 21:34) for *sorgen der narung*, as at [2]; it is kept.

#### [29] Fifth Sunday in Lent (Judica)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 89):** *Am Sontag nach Mitfasten/Johannis am 8.* [On the Sunday after Mid-Lent. John 8.]

**Assignment:** Fifth Sunday in Lent. TLHy has no collect at all for this Sunday.

**German (PDF pp. 91–92)**

> HErr Got himlischer Vatter/dein lieber Sun vnnd vnser erlöser Christus Jesus sagt: Wer mein wort helt/wirdt den todt nicht sehen ewigklich/Wir bitten dich gibe deinen heyligen Geyst in vnsere hertzen/weyl du zu deines Suns wort vns kommen/vnnd das selb hast hören lassen/das wirs auch halten/von gantzem hertzen vns drauff verlassen/vnd mitten im tod desselben vns trösten/vnnd den todt in ewigkeyt nicht sehen/vmb deines lieben Suns/vnnd vnsers erlösers Christi Jhesu willen/AMEN.

**New translation**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, Thy dear Son and our Redeemer, Jesus Christ, saith: "Whosoever keepeth My word shall never see death." We beseech Thee, give Thy Holy Ghost into our hearts, that, since Thou hast brought us to the word of Thy Son and hast caused us to hear it, we may also keep it, rely upon it with our whole heart, comfort ourselves therewith in the midst of death, and never in eternity see death; for the sake of Thy dear Son and our Redeemer, Jesus Christ, *who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Notes**
- **The quotation.** It is John 8:51.
- **A reason, not a request.** *weyl du … vns kommen/vnnd das selb hast hören lassen* gives the ground of the petition ("since Thou hast…"); it is not itself a petition.
- **Ending.** Dietrich's own close, "for the sake of Thy dear Son and our Redeemer, Jesus Christ," is continued with TLHy's "who liveth and reigneth…" (§2.4d).

#### [30] Palm Sunday

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 92):** *Am Palm Sontag/vom leyden vnsers Herren Christi/Esa. 53.* [On Palm Sunday: of the suffering of our Lord Christ. Isaiah 53.]

**Assignment:** Palm Sunday. TLHy's collect for that day is not Dietrich's ("Almighty and everlasting God, who hast caused Thy beloved Son to take our nature upon Himself…"; Appendix A, [31]).

**German (PDF pp. 95–96)**

> HErr Gott himlischer Vatter/der du auß väterlicher gnade/deines eingebornen Sunes nit verschonet/sonder in den todt vnd an das Creutz hinan geben hast/Wir bitten dich/gib deinen heyligen Geyst in vnsere hertzen) [sic: ")" for virgule] das wir solcher gnad vns hertzlich trösten/für sünden ferner vns hüten/vnnd was du vns zu leyden zuschickest/gedultig tragen mögen/auff das wir durch jhn/mit dir ewigklich leben/Amen.

**New translation**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, who of fatherly grace didst not spare Thine only-begotten Son, but didst deliver Him up unto death and to the cross: We beseech Thee, give Thy Holy Ghost into our hearts, that we may heartily comfort ourselves with such grace, guard ourselves henceforth against sin, and patiently bear whatever Thou sendest us to suffer; that we may live with Thee eternally through Him, *who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Notes**
- **The text.** Dietrich's Palm Sunday text is Isaiah 53. *nit verschonet … hinan geben* echoes Romans 8:32.
- **Ending.** Dietrich's closing "through Him" is moved to the end of its clause so that TLHy's "who liveth and reigneth…" can follow it; only the word order changes (§2.4c).

#### [32] Maundy Thursday

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 96):** *Von dem Abendmal des Herren 1. Cor. 11.* [Of the Lord's Supper. 1 Corinthians 11.]

**Relation to TLHy.** TLHy's Maundy Thursday collect (Stratman's [33], which he marks as TLHy's alone) is a free adaptation of this prayer, readdressed to Christ: its thanksgiving for the sacrament, "that we may not receive this gift unworthily," the confession of sins, faith in forgiveness and growth "day by day … in faith and love, until we obtain eternal salvation" all come from Dietrich. It is therefore revised here against Dietrich's German, and the revision serves as the English of [32].

**German (PDF p. 100)**

> HErr Got himlischer Vatter/wir dancken dir für dein grosse gnade/das du durch deinen Son diß Abendmal hast verordnet/da wir sein Leyb essen/vnnd sein Blut trincken/Wir bitten dich/gib durch deinen heiligen Geyst/das wir solche gabe nicht vnwirdig brauchen/sonder vnsere sünde erkennen/vnd dauon ablassen/vñ durch Christum vergebung der selben hoffen vnd glauben/vnd also im glauben vñ liebe von tag zu tag zunemen/biß wir ewig selig werden/Durch Christum Jesum deinen Son vnnd vnseren Herren/AMEN.

**Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, we thank Thee for Thy great grace, that through Thy Son Thou hast instituted this Supper, in which we eat His body and drink His blood: Grant us, we beseech Thee, by Thy Holy Ghost, that we may not receive this gift unworthily, but that we may acknowledge our sins and forsake them, through Christ hope for and believe the forgiveness of the same, and so day by day grow in faith and love, until we obtain eternal salvation; through Jesus Christ, Thy Son and our Lord, *who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**
- Address: "O Lord Jesus Christ" → "Lord God, heavenly Father" (*HErr Got himlischer Vatter*). Dietrich prays to the Father, and the rest of the prayer is turned accordingly.
- "that of Thine infinite mercy Thou hast instituted this Thy sacrament, in which we eat Thy body and drink Thy blood" → "for Thy great grace, that through Thy Son Thou hast instituted this Supper, in which we eat His body and drink His blood" (*für dein grosse gnade/das du durch deinen Son diß Abendmal hast verordnet/da wir sein Leyb essen/vnnd sein Blut trincken*).
- "but that we may confess our sins, remember Thine agony and death, believe the forgiveness of sin" → "but that we may acknowledge our sins and forsake them, through Christ hope for and believe the forgiveness of the same" (*sonder vnsere sünde erkennen/vnd dauon ablassen/vñ durch Christum vergebung der selben hoffen vnd glauben*). "Remember Thine agony and death" is not in the German.
- "and day by day grow" → "and so day by day grow" (*vnd also ... von tag zu tag zunemen*).
- "until we obtain eternal salvation through Thee, who livest and reignest with the Father and the Holy Ghost" → "until we obtain eternal salvation; through Jesus Christ, Thy Son and our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost," which continues Dietrich's own close (*Durch Christum Jesum deinen Son vnnd vnseren Herren*; §2.4d).
- "Holy Ghost" for "Holy Spirit."

**Note.** *Brauchen* is "use," as in the Lutheran "use" of the sacrament; TLHy's "receive … unworthily" (cf. 1 Corinthians 11:27) is kept as an equivalent.
