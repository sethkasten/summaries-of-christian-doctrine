### The Trinity season

#### [47] First Sunday after Trinity

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 143):** *Am ersten Sontag/vom reychen Mann/vnd armen Lazaro/Luce am 16.* [On the First Sunday: of the rich man and poor Lazarus. Luke 16.]

**German (PDF pp. 145–146)**

> HErr Gott himlischer Vatter/Wir bitten dich/du wöllest vnsere hertzē durch deinen heyligen Geyst/also füren vnd regieren/das wir dein liebes wort/nit/wie der reyche Man/one frucht hören/vnd mit dem zeytlichen gut nit also vmbgehn/das wir des ewigen dabey vergessen/sonder armen leuten gern vñ miltigklich nach vnserm vermögen helffen/vnd mit hoffart vnd vberfluß vns nit versündigen/auch wo wir mit dem Creutz vnnd vnglück bedranget werden/nicht verzagen/sonder alle hoffnung an deiner ewigen hilffe vnd genad haben/vnd in gedult alles vberwinden. Amen.

**Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, we beseech Thee so to direct and govern our hearts by Thy Holy Ghost, that we may not, like the rich man, hear Thy dear word without fruit, nor so occupy ourselves with temporal goods as to forget things eternal; but that we readily and liberally, according to our ability, minister to such as are in need, and sin not through pride and excess; and that, when we are oppressed with cross and misfortune, we may not despair, but put all our hope in Thine eternal help and grace, and in patience overcome all things; *through Thy Son, Jesus Christ, our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**
- "so to rule and govern our hearts by Thy Holy Spirit" → "so to direct and govern our hearts by Thy Holy Ghost" (*vnsere hertzē durch deinen heyligen Geyst/also füren vnd regieren*).
- "hear Thy word in vain" → "hear Thy dear word without fruit" (*dein liebes wort ... one frucht hören*). TLHy has "in vain" here and "without fruit" at [48], where the German has the reverse.
- "and become so devoted to things temporal as to forget things eternal" → "nor so occupy ourselves with temporal goods as to forget things eternal" (*vnd mit dem zeytlichen gut nit also vmbgehn/das wir des ewigen dabey vergessen*).
- "readily and according to our ability" → "readily and liberally, according to our ability" (*gern vñ miltigklich nach vnserm vermögen*).
- "and not defile ourselves with surfeiting and pride" → "and sin not through pride and excess" (*vnd mit hoffart vnd vberfluß vns nit versündigen*).
- "in trial and misfortune keep us from despair" → "and that, when we are oppressed with cross and misfortune, we may not despair" (*auch wo wir mit dem Creutz vnnd vnglück bedranget werden/nicht verzagen*). Dietrich continues the petition; TLHy turns it into a new request.
- "and grant us to put our trust wholly in Thy fatherly help and grace" → "but put all our hope in Thine eternal help and grace" (*sonder alle hoffnung an deiner ewigen hilffe vnd genad haben*).
- "so that in faith and Christian patience we may overcome all things" → "and in patience overcome all things" (*vnd in gedult alles vberwinden*). "In faith and Christian" is not in the German. Dietrich gives no conclusion, so TLHy's ending is kept in italics.

#### [48] Second Sunday after Trinity

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 146):** *Am anderē Sontag/nach Trinitatis/das gleychnuß vom grossen Abendmal/Luce. 14.* [On the Second Sunday after Trinity: the parable of the great supper. Luke 14.]

**German (PDF p. 149)**

> HErr Got himlischer Vatter/wir dancken dir/das du vns zu deiner Malzeit dem ewigen leben/durch dein liebes wort hast lassen laden/Vñ bitten dich/du wöllest durch deinen heyligen Geyst vnsere hertzen erwecken/das wir dein wort nicht vergeblich hören/vns zu solcher Malzeyt recht schicken/vnd das zeytliche vns nit dran hindern lassen/AMEN.

**Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, we give thanks unto Thee, that through Thy dear word Thou hast called us to Thy supper, even to eternal life, and we beseech Thee: Quicken our hearts by Thy Holy Ghost, that we may not hear Thy word in vain, but that we may prepare ourselves rightly for such a supper, and not suffer ourselves to be hindered therein by things temporal; *through Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ, our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**

- "to Thy great supper" → "to Thy supper, even to eternal life" (*zu deiner Malzeit dem ewigen leben*). "Great" comes from the Gospel (Luke 14:16), not the prayer; Dietrich names the supper as eternal life.

- "for Thy kingdom" → "for such a supper" (*zu solcher Malzeyt*).
- "by any worldly care" → "therein by things temporal" (*vnd das zeytliche vns nit dran hindern lassen*).
- "Holy Ghost" for "Holy Spirit"; the OLM web text's "Son. Jesus Christ" corrected to "Son, Jesus Christ."

#### [49] Third Sunday after Trinity

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 149):** *Am dritten Sontag nach Trinitatis/von dem verlornen schefflein/welchem der Hirt nachgehet/vnd es suchet/Luc. 19. [sic, for Luke 15]* [On the Third Sunday after Trinity: of the lost sheep, which the shepherd goes after and seeks. Luke 15.]

**German (PDF p. 152)**

> HErr Got himlischer Vatter/wir sind leyder wie die Schaffe in der jrre/vnnd haben vns durch den Sathan/vnnd vnser sündhafftes fleysch vom rechten wege lassen abfüren/wir bitten dich aber/sey gnedig/vnd vergib alle vnsere sünde/vmb deines Sones Christi Jesu willen/vnd erwecke vnsere hertzen durch deinen heyligen Geyst/das wir an deinem wort fest halten/vnnd im Schaffstal deyner Christlichen Kirchen/in rechter Buß vnd warem glauben/biß ans ende beharren/vnd selig werden/ANEN. **Changes from TLHy**

- "but willingly forgive all men" → "but willingly forgive and give to all men" (*yederman gern vergeben/vnd geben*); TLHy omits "and give."
- The OLM web text's "Judging" corrected to "judging." Dietrich gives no conclusion, so TLHy's ending is kept in italics.

#### [52] Sixth Sunday after Trinity

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 160):** *Am sechsten Sontag nach Trinitatis/ein lehr/was Christliche gerechtigkeyt sey/Matth. 5.* [On the Sixth Sunday after Trinity: a teaching on what Christian righteousness is. Matthew 5.]

> HERR Gott himlischer Vatter/wir bekennen das wir leyder arme/ellende sünder sint/da gar nichts gutes an ist/Deñ vnser hertz/fleysch vnd blut/ist durch die sünde dermassen vergifftet/das wir der bösen lüste nymmer können on sein. #### [54] Eighth Sunday after Trinity

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 167):** *Am achten Sontag nach Trinitatis/warnung der falschen Propheten halb/Mathei am 7.* [On the Eighth Sunday after Trinity: a warning concerning false prophets. Matthew 7.]

> HERR Gott hymelischer Vatter/Wir dancken dir von hertzen/das du vns hast lassen zum erkentnuß deines lieben worts kommen/Wir bitten dich aber auch/du wöllest vns dabey gnedig erhalten/vnd seligklich drinn sterben lassen/wöllest auch alle from̃e prediger/so dein wort trewlich fürtragen/für allem ergernuß vnd anderm vnglück/gnedig behüten/vnd jnen jr leben fristen. **Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, we most heartily thank Thee that Thou hast caused us to come to the knowledge of Thy dear word. We pray Thee also: graciously keep us steadfast therein, and let us die blessedly therein; graciously defend also all pious preachers, who faithfully preach Thy word, from all offense and other misfortune, and grant them long life. **Changes from TLHy.** TLHy recasts the second half of this prayer freely.
- "the knowledge of Thy word" → "the knowledge of Thy dear word" (*zum erkentnuß deines lieben worts*).
- "We pray Thee: graciously keep us steadfast in this knowledge unto death, that we may obtain eternal life" → "We pray Thee also: graciously keep us steadfast therein, and let us die blessedly therein" (*Wir bitten dich aber auch/du wöllest vns dabey gnedig erhalten/vnd seligklich drinn sterben lassen*), as at [13].
- "send us now and ever pious pastors, who faithfully preach Thy word, without offense or false doctrine, and grant them long life" → "graciously defend also all pious preachers, who faithfully preach Thy word, from all offense and other misfortune, and grant them long life" (*wöllest auch alle from̃e prediger/so dein wort trewlich fürtragen/für allem ergernuß vnd anderm vnglück/gnedig behüten/vnd jnen jr leben fristen*). - "that Thy true Church may evermore be established among us, and be defended and preserved from such false teachers" → "and graciously defend Thy poor Christendom from them" (*Vnd dein arme Christenheyt gnedig für jnen behüten*).
- "Through Jesus Christ, Thy Son" is Dietrich's own close (*durch Christum Jesum deinen Son*, rendered "Jesus Christ" as in §2.2); TLHy already has it, and the conclusion continues it (§2.4d).

#### [57] Eleventh Sunday after Trinity

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 176):** *Am eylfften sontag/vom Phariseer vnd Zölner/wie sie bede im Tempel betten/Luc. 18.* [On the Eleventh Sunday: of the Pharisee and the publican, how they both pray in the Temple. Luke 18.]

**German (PDF p. 179)**

> HErr Gott himlischer Vatter/Jch bitte dich/du wöllest durch deinen heyligen Geyst/mich also leyten vñ füren/das ich erstlich meiner sünde nicht vergesse/noch sicher werde/sonder in steter busse stehe/vnd mich von tag zu tag bessere/vnd darnach allein mich dessen tröste/das du vmb deines Suns Christi Jhesu willen/mir genedig sein/alle sünde vergeben/vnd selig wilt machen/AMEN.

**Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, I beseech Thee so to guide and direct me by Thy Holy Ghost, that, first, I may not forget my sin, nor grow secure, but continue in constant repentance, and amend my life from day to day; and then comfort myself with this alone, that for the sake of Thy Son, Jesus Christ, Thou wilt be gracious unto me, forgive me all my sins, and save me; *through Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ, our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Congregational form (TLHy practice)**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, we beseech Thee so to guide and direct us by Thy Holy Ghost, that, first, we may not forget our sin, nor grow secure, but continue in constant repentance, and amend our lives from day to day; and then comfort ourselves with this alone, that for the sake of Thy Son, Jesus Christ, Thou wilt be gracious unto us, forgive us all our sins, and save us; *through Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ, our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**
- Person: Dietrich's prayer is in the first person singular ("I"); TLHy makes it plural. The revised text follows Dietrich, and a congregational form is supplied (§2.2).
- "that we may not forget our sins and be filled with pride" → "that, first, I may not forget my sin, nor grow secure" (*das ich erstlich meiner sünde nicht vergesse/noch sicher werde*). Dietrich's word is carnal security, not pride.
- "but continue in daily repentance and renewal" → "but continue in constant repentance, and amend my life from day to day" (*sonder in steter busse stehe/vnd mich von tag zu tag bessere*).
- "seeking our comfort only in the blessed knowledge that Thou wilt be merciful unto us" → "and then comfort myself with this alone, that for the sake of Thy Son, Jesus Christ, Thou wilt be gracious unto me" (*vnd darnach allein mich dessen tröste/das du vmb deines Suns Christi Jhesu willen/mir genedig sein*). TLHy omits "for the sake of Thy Son."
- "forgive us our sins, and grant us eternal life" → "forgive me all my sins, and save me" (*alle sünde vergeben/vnd selig wilt machen*). Dietrich gives no conclusion, so TLHy's ending is kept in italics.
- "Holy Ghost" for "Holy Spirit."

#### [58] Twelfth Sunday after Trinity

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 180):** *Am zwölfftē Sontag/wie vnser Herr Jesus einen stummen/vnd tauben gesund machet/Marci am 7.* [On the Twelfth Sunday: how our Lord Jesus makes a dumb and deaf man whole. Mark 7.]

**German (PDF pp. 182–183)**

> ALmechtiger ewiger Got/du gnediger schöpffer aller ding/Jch dancke dir/das du mir gesunden leyb geben/vnnd meine zungen/ohren/vnd andere glidmaß/genedigklich für dem bösen feind behütet hast/Vnd bitte dich/gibe mir dein gnad/das ich sonderlich meine ohren vñ zunge/recht brauche/Mit meinen ohren dein wort fleyssig hören/vñ wol mercken/vnnd mit meiner zungen/dein gnad preysen vñ rhümen möge/auff das niemandt durch mein zunge geergert/sonder yederman dadurch gebessert werde/Amen.

**Revised TLHy**

> Almighty and everlasting God, Thou gracious Creator of all things, I thank Thee that Thou hast given me a sound body, and hast graciously preserved my tongue, ears, and other members from the evil foe; and I beseech Thee, grant me Thy grace, that I may rightly use especially my ears and tongue: with my ears diligently hear Thy word and mark it well, and with my tongue praise and magnify Thy grace, that no one may be offended by my tongue, but that all may be edified thereby; *through Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ, our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Congregational form (TLHy practice)**

> Almighty and everlasting God, Thou gracious Creator of all things, we thank Thee that Thou hast given us sound bodies, and hast graciously preserved our tongues, ears, and other members from the evil foe; and we beseech Thee, grant us Thy grace, that we may rightly use especially our ears and tongues: with our ears diligently hear Thy word and mark it well, and with our tongues praise and magnify Thy grace, that no one may be offended by our tongues, but that all may be edified thereby; *through Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ, our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**
- Person: Dietrich's prayer is in the first person singular ("I"); TLHy makes it plural. The revised text follows Dietrich, and a congregational form is supplied (§2.2).
- "who hast created all things" → "Thou gracious Creator of all things" (*du gnediger schöpffer aller ding*).
- "our tongues and other members" → "my tongue, ears, and other members" (*meine zungen/ohren/vnd andere glidmaß*); TLHy omits "ears."
- "from the power of the adversary" → "from the evil foe" (*für dem bösen feind*).
- "that we may rightly use our ears and tongues" → "that I may rightly use especially my ears and tongue" (*das ich sonderlich meine ohren vñ zunge/recht brauche*).

- "offended by our words" → "offended by my tongue" (*durch mein zunge geergert*). Dietrich gives no conclusion, so TLHy's ending is kept in italics.
