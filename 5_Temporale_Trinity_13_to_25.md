- "so quicken our hearts" → "so lead and quicken our hearts" (*vnsere hertzen also füren vnd erwecken*).
- "that we in daily prayer may seek our help in Christ against all temptations" → "that we may seek such help from Him in daily prayer, and especially in all temptations" (*das wir solche hilff durch tegliches gebet/vnd sonderlich inn allen anfechtung bey jm suchen*).
- "and, constantly believing His promise, obtain that for which we pray" → "and through a right and firm faith in His promise and word surely find and attain it" (*vnd durch ein rechten festen glauben/auff sein zusagung vñ wort/gewiß finden vñ erlangen*).
- "and at last be saved, through Thy Son Jesus Christ," → "and at last obtain eternal salvation through Him," which is Dietrich's phrase (*Vnd endlich durch jn ewig selig werden*); the conclusion follows (§2.4c).
- "Holy Ghost" for "Holy Spirit."

#### [68] Twenty-second Sunday after Trinity

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 214):** *Am zwey vñ zwentzigsten Sontag/Das gleychnuß vom knecht der seym Herrn zehen tausent pfund schuldig war/vnnd hette nicht zubezalen/Matth. 18.* [On the Twenty-second Sunday: the parable of the servant who owed his lord ten thousand pounds and had nothing to pay. Matthew 18.]

**German (PDF pp. 216–217)**

> ALmechtiger ewiger Got/wir erkennen/das wir arme sünder sind/vnnd tieff in deinem regiester stehen/Wir dancken dir aber von hertzen/das du solche schulde von vns gekommen [sic, for genommen]/auff deinen lieben Son/Christum Jesum geleget/vnnd jn für vns hast bezalen lassen. Der mit dir vnd dem heiligen Geyst/lebet vnd regieret/von ewigkeyt zu ewigkeyt/AMEN.

**Revised TLHy**

> Almighty, eternal God: We acknowledge that we are poor sinners, and deep in Thy debt; but with all our hearts we thank Thee, that Thou hast taken this debt from us and laid it upon Thy dear Son, Jesus Christ, and made Him pay it for us: And we pray Thee also graciously to keep us in this faith, and by Thy Holy Ghost so to guide and direct us here on earth, that we may live according to Thy will, and gladly show our neighbor all love, service, and help; and, on the other hand, put away all anger, impatience, and vindictiveness, that we may not provoke Thy wrath against us, but always find in Thee a gracious Father through Jesus Christ, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, world without end. Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**
- "O almighty, eternal God" → "Almighty, eternal God" (*ALmechtiger ewiger Got*).
- "We confess that we are poor sinners and cannot answer one of a thousand, when Thou contendest with us" → "We acknowledge that we are poor sinners, and deep in Thy debt" (*wir erkennen/das wir arme sünder sind/vnnd tieff in deinem regiester stehen*, literally "stand deep in Thy register"). TLHy substitutes Job 9:3 for Dietrich's image of the debt book, which belongs to the parable of the day.
- "all our guilt" → "this debt" (*solche schulde*).
- "and made Him to atone for it" → "and made Him pay it for us" (*vnnd jn für vns hast bezalen lassen*).
- "We pray Thee graciously to sustain us in faith" → "And we pray Thee also graciously to keep us in this faith" (*Vnnd bitten dich auch/du wöllest in solchem glauben vns gnedig erhalten*).
- "and so to govern us by Thy Holy Spirit" → "and by Thy Holy Ghost so to guide and direct us here on earth" (*vnd durch deinen heyligen Geyst/hie auff erden also leyten vnd füren*).
- "in neighborly love, service, and helpfulness" → "and gladly show our neighbor all love, service, and help" (*vnd vnserm nechsten alle lieb/dienst vnnd hilffe gern beweysen*).
- "and not give way to wrath or revenge" → "and, on the other hand, put away all anger, impatience, and vindictiveness" (*Widerumb/allen zorn/vngedult vnnd rachgirigkeyt fallen lassen*).
- "that we may not incur Thy wrath" → "that we may not provoke Thy wrath against us" (*auff das wir deinen zoren wider vns nicht erregen*).
- The conclusion is Dietrich's own (*durch Christum Jesum ... Der mit dir vnd dem heiligen Geyst/lebet vnd regieret/von ewigkeyt zu ewigkeyt*). TLHy's "our Lord" and "one true God" are not in it and are dropped; nothing is added (§2.4g).
- "Holy Ghost" for "Holy Spirit."

#### [69] Twenty-third Sunday after Trinity

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 217):** *Am drey vnd zwentzigsten Sontag/wie die Phariseer Christum versuchen/ob man dem Keyser soll den zinß geben/Matth. 22.* [On the Twenty-third Sunday: how the Pharisees tempt Christ, whether one should give tribute to Caesar. Matthew 22.]

**German (PDF p. 220)**

> HErr Got himlischer Vatter/Wir dancken dir/das du bißher genedigen frid geben/vnd für krieg vnd frembder Herrschafft vns Vätterlich behütet hast/Wir bitten dich/gib dein gnad ferner/das wir in deiner forcht/vnnd nach deim willen leben/vnnd durch sünde nicht vrsach zu krieg/vnnd andern der gleychen straffen geben. Regiere vnd füre vnser Oberkeit also/das sie deinen gehorsam nit hindere/sonder fürdere/vnd wir dest mehr segens vnd glücks/vnter jrem Regiment haben mögen/AMEN.

**Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father: we thank Thee that Thou hast hitherto granted us gracious peace, and as a father hast protected us from war and foreign dominion: We pray Thee, give us Thy grace henceforth, that we may live in Thy fear and according to Thy will, and by sin give no cause for war and other like punishments; govern and direct our magistrates, that they may not hinder the obedience due to Thee, but further it, and that we may enjoy the more happiness and blessing under their government; *through our Lord Jesus Christ, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**
- "granted us peace and graciously spared us" → "granted us gracious peace, and as a father hast protected us" (*genedigen frid geben/vnd ... vns Vätterlich behütet hast*).

- "giving no cause for wars or other punishment" → "and by sin give no cause for war and other like punishments" (*vnnd durch sünde nicht vrsach zu krieg/vnnd andern der gleychen straffen geben*).
- "but maintain righteousness" → "but further it" (*sonder fürdere*), that is, the obedience due to God.

#### [71] Twenty-fifth Sunday after Trinity
