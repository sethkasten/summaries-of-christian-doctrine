## PART I. TEMPORALE

St. Stephen's Day, which the *Summaria* prints after Christmas, is given with the saints' days in Part II ([81]).

### Advent

#### [1] First Sunday in Advent

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 13):** *Am ersten Sontag des Aduents/wie Christus zu Jerusalem auff eim Esel einreytet/Euang. Matth. 21.* [On the First Sunday in Advent: how Christ rides into Jerusalem on an ass. Gospel, Matthew 21.]

**German (PDF pp. 15–16)**

> HErr Gott himlischer Vatter/wir dancken dir ye billich/vnd loben/vnd preysen dich inn ewigkeyt/das du vns armen menschen/so wir der sünden halb/vnter des todtes vnnd Sathans Tyranney haben müssen sein/deinen Son zu einem König gesetzet hast/der ein gerechter König vñ Heyland sein/das ist/vns von vnsern sünden/durch seinen todt ledig machen/vnnd wider den todt retten vnd helffen soll/Wir bitten dich auch/du wöllest durch deinen heyligen Geyst vns erleuchten/regieren vnnd füren/das wir an disen gerechten König vnd Heyland vns alleine [type damaged] halten/vnd an seiner armen gestalt vñ verachtetem wort vns nit/wie die welt/ergern/vnd also durch ein festes vertrawen auff jn/ewig selig werden/AMEN.

**Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, we thank Thee, as is meet and right, we bless and praise Thee forever, that over us poor men, who because of sin had to be under the tyranny of death and Satan, Thou hast set Thy Son as King, to be a righteous King and Savior, that is, by His death to set us free from our sins, and to rescue and help us against death: We beseech Thee also so to enlighten, govern and direct us by Thy Holy Ghost, that we may hold to this righteous King and Savior alone, and not, after the manner of the world, be offended at His humble form and despised word, and so, through a firm trust in Him, obtain eternal salvation; *through the same, Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ, our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**
- "we thank Thee" → "we thank Thee, as is meet and right," restoring *ye billich*.
- TLHy's "that Thou didst send Thy Son to rule over us poor sinners, who for our transgressions did justly deserve to remain in the bondage of sin and Satan, and didst give us in Him a meek and righteous King, who by His death became our Savior from sin and eternal death" is replaced by a rendering of the German. Dietrich speaks of "poor men" (*armen menschen*), not "sinners"; of men who "had to be" (*haben müssen sein*), not who "did justly deserve to remain"; and of "the tyranny of death and Satan" (*des todtes vnnd Sathans Tyranney*), not "the bondage of sin and Satan". "Meek" is not in the prayer. The Son is set as King "to be" Savior, "that is," to free us from sin by His death and to help us against death (*der ein gerechter König vñ Heyland sein/das ist/vns von vnsern sünden/durch seinen todt ledig machen/vnnd wider den todt retten vnd helffen soll*).
- "We beseech Thee so" → "We beseech Thee also so" (*auch*).
- "ever remain faithful to this righteous King and Savior" → "hold to this righteous King and Savior alone" (*vns alleine halten*).
- "with His humble form" → "at His humble form" (idiom only).
- "but, firmly believing in Him," → "and so, through a firm trust in Him," (*vnd also durch ein festes vertrawen auff jn*).
- "Holy Ghost" for "Holy Spirit."

#### [2] Second Sunday in Advent

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 16):** *Am andern Sontag des Aduents/was für zeychen vor dem Jüngsten tag kommen/vnd wie die Christen dazu sich schicken sollen/Luce am 22.* [On the Second Sunday in Advent: what signs come before the Last Day, and how Christians should prepare themselves for it. Luke 22 [sic, for 21].]

**German (PDF pp. 18–19)**

> HERR Gott hymlischer Vatter/der du durch deinen Son vnns hast offenbaret/wie Hymel vnd erden zergehn/vnser leybe wider aufferstehen/vnd wir alle für Gericht sollen gestellet werden/Wir bitten dich/du wöllest durch deinen heiligen geyst vns in deim wort/vnd rechtem glauben erhalten/für sünden genedig behüten/vnd in aller anfechtung erhalten/das wir vnsere hertzen mit fressen vnd sauffen/vnd sorgen der Narung nicht beschweren/sonder allzeyt wacker sein/vnd beten/vnd in gewiser zuuersicht deiner gnad/der zukunfft deines Sons mit frewden erwarten/vnd durch jn ewig selig werden/Amen.

**Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, who by Thy Son hast revealed to us that heaven and earth shall pass away, that our bodies shall rise again, and that we all shall appear before the judgment seat: We beseech Thee, keep us by Thy Holy Ghost in Thy word and in the true faith, graciously defend us from sin and preserve us in all temptations, that our hearts may not be overcharged with surfeiting and drunkenness, and cares of this life, but that we may ever watch and pray, and in sure confidence of Thy grace await with joy the coming of Thy Son, and obtain eternal salvation through Him, *who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**
- "keep us by Thy Holy Spirit in Thy word; establish us in the true faith" → "keep us by Thy Holy Ghost in Thy word and in the true faith." The German has one verb for both (*vns in deim wort/vnd rechtem glauben erhalten*); "establish" is not in it.
- "trusting fully in Thy grace" → "in sure confidence of Thy grace" (*in gewiser zuuersicht deiner gnad*).
- "the glorious coming" → "the coming"; "glorious" is not in the German.
- "and at last obtain eternal salvation, through Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ our Lord," → "and obtain eternal salvation through Him," (*vnd durch jn ewig selig werden*; "at last" is not in the German). TLHy's conclusion follows (§2.4c).
- "Holy Ghost" for "Holy Spirit."

**Note.** "Overcharged with surfeiting and drunkenness, and cares of this life" is TLHy's King James wording of Luke 21:34, which Dietrich's *fressen vnd sauffen/vnd sorgen der Narung* follows in Luther's version; it is kept.

#### [3] Third Sunday in Advent

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 20):** *Am dritten Sontag des Aduents/wie Johannes seine Jüngern zu Christo schicket/Matth. 11.* [On the Third Sunday in Advent: how John sends his disciples to Christ. Matthew 11.]

**German (PDF p. 22)**

> HErr Got hymlischer Vatter/der du deinen Son vnsern herrn Jesum Christum mensch werden/vnd darumb inn dise welt hast kommen lassen/das er des Teuffels Tyranney wehren/vns armen menschen wider sünd vnd tod helffen/vnd ewig soll selig machē/Wir bitten dich/du wöllest mit deinem heiligen Geist/vnsere hertzen also füren vnd leyten/das wir auff anders nichts/denn auff sein wort sehen/vnd also allem ergernuß/da vnser vernunfft vnns auffweyset/empfliehen/vnnd vnter dem heufflein mögen gefunden werden/welcher sich an deinem Son Christo Jesu nicht ergert/sonder durch jn selig wirdt/Amen.

**Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, who didst suffer Thy Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, to become man, and for this cause to come into this world, that He should restrain the tyranny of the devil, help us poor men against sin and death, and save us eternally: We beseech Thee so to lead and guide our hearts with Thy Holy Ghost, that we may look to nothing else but His word, and so avoid all offense whereunto our reason directeth us, and may be found among the little company which is not offended in Thy Son, Jesus Christ, but is saved through Him, *who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**
- "and to come into the world" → "and for this cause to come into this world" (*vnd darumb inn dise welt*).
- "that He might destroy the works of the devil" → "that He should restrain the tyranny of the devil" (*des Teuffels Tyranney wehren*).
- "deliver us poor offenders from sin and death" → "help us poor men against sin and death" (*vns armen menschen wider sünd vnd tod helffen*).
- "and give us everlasting life" → "and save us eternally" (*vnd ewig soll selig machen*).
- "so to rule and govern our hearts by Thy Holy Spirit" → "so to lead and guide our hearts with Thy Holy Ghost" (*mit deinem heiligen Geist ... füren vnd leyten*).
- "that we may seek no other refuge than His word" → "that we may look to nothing else but His word" (*auff anders nichts/denn auff sein wort sehen*).
- "and thus avoid all offense to which, by nature, we are inclined" → "and so avoid all offense whereunto our reason directeth us" (*da vnser vernunfft vnns auffweyset*).
- "in order that we may always be found among the faithful followers of Thy Son, Jesus Christ, and by faith in Him obtain eternal salvation" → "and may be found among the little company which is not offended in Thy Son, Jesus Christ, but is saved through Him" (*vnter dem heufflein ... welcher sich an deinem Son Christo Jesu nicht ergert/sonder durch jn selig wirdt*). "Offended in" is the King James idiom of Matthew 11:6, the Gospel of the day.
- "through the same, Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ our Lord," is dropped, since Dietrich's last clause already ends "through Him"; TLHy's conclusion follows it (§2.4c).

#### [4] Fourth Sunday in Advent

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 23):** *Am vierdten Sontag des Aduents/wie die Juden von Jerusalem zu Johanne schicken/vnd jhm das tauffen verbieten wöllen/Johannis am 1.* [On the Fourth Sunday in Advent: how the Jews send from Jerusalem to John and would forbid him to baptize. John 1.]

**German (PDF pp. 25–26)**

> HErr Gott himlischer Vatter/wir dancken dir ye billich/das du die selige gnadenreyche Tauf/durch den heyligen Tauffer Johannem hast erstlich angerichtet/vnnd vns auch dazu lassen kommen/In welcher du vns vmb deines Suns Christi Jesu willen zusagest vergebung der sünden/den heyligen Geyst/vnd das ewige leben/Wir bitten dich/erhalte vns inn solchem vertrawen auff dein gnad vnd barmhertzigkeyt/das wir an solcher zusagung nymmer mehr zweyffelen/sonder der selben inn allerley anfechtung vns trösten/Vnd gib durch deinen heyligen Geyst/das wir für sünden vns hüten/vnnd in solcher vnschuld/dazu wir durch die Tauffe kommen sind/bleyben mögen/Oder ja wo wir fallen/vnnd menschlicher schwachheyt halb nicht können bestehen/das wir inn sünden nicht ligen bleyben/sonder durch rechtschaffene Buß vns bekeren/vnnd deiner gnad vnnd zusagung/vns wider trösten/vnd also durch dein genad ewig selig werden/AMEN.

**Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, it is meet and right that we should give thanks unto Thee, that Thou didst first establish the blessed baptism, rich in grace, through holy John the Baptist, and hast caused us also to come unto it, wherein Thou dost promise us, for the sake of Thy Son, Jesus Christ, the remission of sins, the Holy Ghost, and everlasting life: Preserve us, we beseech Thee, in such trust in Thy grace and mercy, that we may never doubt Thy promise, but be comforted by the same in all temptations; and grant by Thy Holy Ghost that we may guard ourselves against sin, and continue in the innocence unto which we have come through baptism; or, if we fall, and by reason of human weakness cannot stand, that we may not lie still in sin, but turn again through true repentance, and comfort ourselves again with Thy grace and promise, and so through Thy grace obtain eternal salvation; *through the same, Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**
- "that Thou hast given us a more glorious baptism than that of John the Baptist" → "that Thou didst first establish the blessed baptism, rich in grace, through holy John the Baptist, and hast caused us also to come unto it" (*das du die selige gnadenreyche Tauf/durch den heyligen Tauffer Johannem hast erstlich angerichtet/vnnd vns auch dazu lassen kommen*). Dietrich does not set our baptism above John's; he says it was first instituted through John.
- "and hast therein promised us the remission of sins, the Holy Spirit, and everlasting life through Thy Son, Jesus Christ" → "wherein Thou dost promise us, for the sake of Thy Son, Jesus Christ, the remission of sins, the Holy Ghost, and everlasting life" (*vmb deines Suns Christi Jesu willen*, "for the sake of," not "through").
- "in such faith in Thy grace" → "in such trust in Thy grace" (*vertrawen*).
- "and grant us Thy Holy Spirit that we may renounce sin, and ever continue in the righteousness bestowed upon us in baptism" → "and grant by Thy Holy Ghost that we may guard ourselves against sin, and continue in the innocence unto which we have come through baptism" (*das wir für sünden vns hüten/vnnd in solcher vnschuld/dazu wir durch die Tauffe kommen sind/bleyben mögen*).
- Restored the clause TLHy omits: "or, if we fall, and by reason of human weakness cannot stand, that we may not lie still in sin, but turn again through true repentance, and comfort ourselves again with Thy grace and promise" (*Oder ja wo wir fallen ... vns wider trösten*).
- "until by Thy grace we obtain eternal salvation" → "and so through Thy grace obtain eternal salvation" (*vnd also durch dein genad ewig selig werden*).
- "Holy Ghost" for "Holy Spirit" (twice).

### Christmas

#### [5] Christmas Day

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 27):** *Am heyligen Christage/von der geburt Christi/Luce am 2.* [On holy Christmas Day: of the birth of Christ. Luke 2.]

**German (PDF p. 29)**

> HErr Gott himlischer Vatter/wir dancken dir deiner grossen gnad vñ barmhertzigkeyt/das du deinen eingebornen Sun inn vnser fleisch kommen/vnd durch jn/vns von sünden vnd dem ewigen tod/genediglich hast helffen lassen/Vnd bitten dich/erleuchte vnsere hertzen/durch deinen heyligen Geyst/das wir für solche deine genad dir danckbar sind/vnnd der selben vns in allerley not vnnd anfechtung trösten/vnd also durch deinen Sun/vnseren Herren Jhesum Christum/ewig selig werden/AMEN.

**Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, we give thanks unto Thee for Thy great grace and mercy, that Thou didst suffer Thine only-begotten Son to come into our flesh, and through Him hast graciously helped us out of sin and everlasting death: We beseech Thee, enlighten our hearts by Thy Holy Ghost, that we may be thankful unto Thee for such Thy grace, and comfort ourselves with the same in all tribulation and temptation, and so obtain eternal salvation through Thy Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, *who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**
- "that of Thy mercy and compassion" → "for Thy great grace and mercy, that" (*deiner grossen gnad vñ barmhertzigkeyt*).
- "Thy dear Son to become incarnate" → "Thine only-begotten Son to come into our flesh" (*deinen eingebornen Sun inn vnser fleisch kommen*).
- "and didst through Him redeem us from sin and everlasting death" → "and through Him hast graciously helped us out of sin and everlasting death" (*durch jn/vns von sünden vnd dem ewigen tod/genediglich hast helffen lassen*).
- "that we may ever be thankful for such grace" → "that we may be thankful unto Thee for such Thy grace" ("ever" is not in the German; *dir* restored).
- "and at last obtain eternal salvation through the same, Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ our Lord," → "and so obtain eternal salvation through Thy Son, our Lord Jesus Christ," which is Dietrich's own close (*vnd also durch deinen Sun/vnseren Herren Jhesum Christum*); TLHy's conclusion continues it (§2.4d).
- "Holy Ghost" for "Holy Spirit."

#### [6] First Sunday of Christmas (Sunday after Christmas)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 33):** *Am Sontag nach Weyhenachten/wie Simeon im Tempel Marien vnnd das Kindlin Jesus segnet/vnd von Christo weyssaget/Luce am 2.* [On the Sunday after Christmas: how Simeon in the Temple blesses Mary and the Child Jesus, and prophesies of Christ. Luke 2.]

**Assignment:** TLHy's First Sunday of Christmas. TLHy's only collect for that day is not Dietrich's ("O almighty and everlasting God, mercifully direct our ways…"; Appendix A, [7]).

**German (PDF pp. 35–36)**

> HErr Gott himlischer Vatter/der du durch den heyligen Simeon hast weyßgesaget/wie Christus dein Sun zum fall vnnd aufferstehung viler in Jsrael gesetzt sey/wir bitten dich/erleuchte vnsere hertzen/durch deinen heyligen Geist/das wir deinen Sun Christum Jhesum recht erkennen/vnnd vns in allerley not vnnd fahr/an jn halten/vnd an jm auffstehen/vnd nicht mit der sichern blinden welt vns an jn stossen/auch in allerley leyden vnnd anfechtung/so deines worts vnnd bekentnuß halb vns begegnet/getröst vnnd mütig sein/sintemal gewiß ist/das die/so dein wort verfolgen/zu deim Reych nicht gehören/Die aber dran glauben vnd dabey bleyben/ewig selig werden/AMEN.

**New translation**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, who by holy Simeon didst prophesy that Christ, Thy Son, is set for the fall and rising again of many in Israel: We beseech Thee, enlighten our hearts by Thy Holy Ghost, that we may rightly know Thy Son, Jesus Christ; in all manner of need and danger hold fast unto Him and rise again in Him, and not, with the secure and blind world, stumble at Him; and also, in all manner of suffering and temptation which befalleth us for the sake of Thy word and the confession thereof, be of good comfort and courage: since it is certain that they who persecute Thy word belong not to Thy kingdom, but they who believe it and abide by it shall obtain eternal salvation; *through the same, Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Notes**
- **Wordplay on the text.** The opening follows Luke 2:34 and is given in King James wording ("set for the fall and rising again"). Dietrich then plays on the verse with *an jm auffstehen* ("rise again in Him") and *vns an jn stossen* ("stumble at Him"). Both are kept.
- **"Secure."** *sicher* has the sense TLHy renders elsewhere as "carnal security."
- **"Confession thereof."** *deines worts vnnd bekentnuß halb* is literally "for the sake of Thy word and confession." "The confession thereof" makes the reference explicit.

#### [8] The Circumcision / New Year's Day

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 36):** *Am newen Jarstag/wie das Kindlin beschnitten/vnd Jesus ist genennet worden/Luc. 2.* [On New Year's Day: how the Child was circumcised and named Jesus. Luke 2.]

**Assignment:** TLHy's Circumcision of Christ. TLHy's only collect for that day is not Dietrich's ("O merciful and eternal God, heavenly Father, who didst cause Thy Son to endure circumcision…"; Appendix A, [9]).

**German (PDF pp. 38–39)**

> HErr Gott himlischer Vatter/wir dancken dir für dein Väterliche gnad/das du der armen sünder dich angenommen/vnd deinen Sun/vns zu gut/vnter das gesetz hast gethan/auff das er durch seinen volkommen gehorsam/deinen zorn stillete/vnnd vnsern vngehorsam heylete/Wir bitten dich/du wöllest durch deinen heyligen Geyst/vnsere hertzen also erleuchten/das wir solches gehorsams vns wider vnsere sünde vnnd böses gewissen trösten/vnd durch hilff deines heyligen Geystes/auch anfahen gehorsame kinder zu sein/vnd endlich durch Christum ewig selig werden. Amen.

**New translation**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, we thank Thee for Thy fatherly grace, that Thou hast had compassion upon poor sinners, and for our good hast put Thy Son under the law, that by His perfect obedience He might still Thy wrath and heal our disobedience: We beseech Thee so to enlighten our hearts by Thy Holy Ghost, that we may comfort ourselves with this obedience against our sins and an evil conscience; and that by the help of Thy Holy Ghost we may also begin to be obedient children, and at last obtain eternal salvation through Christ, *who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Notes**
- **Under the law.** *vnter das gesetz hast gethan* echoes Galatians 4:4 (Luther: "vnter das Gesetz gethan"; King James: "made under the law"). The subject is the Father: it is the Father who puts the Son under the law.
- **"Had compassion upon."** *sich … annehmen* means to take up someone's cause or care for them. "Had compassion upon" gives the sense.
- **Whose obedience.** *solches gehorsams vns … trösten* means to comfort ourselves *with this obedience*, that is, Christ's, set against our sin.
- **Ending.** Dietrich's closing "through Christ" is moved to the end of its clause so that TLHy's "who liveth and reigneth…" can follow it; only the word order changes (§2.4c).

#### [10] Second Sunday of Christmas (Sunday after New Year), restored from Quinquagesima

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 40):** *Am Sontag zwischē dem Newen jar vnd Obersten/wie Christus am Jordan von Johanne ist getauffet worden/Matth. 3.* [On the Sunday between New Year and Epiphany: how Christ was baptized by John at the Jordan. Matthew 3.] *Oberster* (Tag) is Dietrich's name for Epiphany.

**Assignment.** TLHy prints this collect at Quinquagesima. It is restored here to the Sunday after New Year, which TLHy calls the Second Sunday of Christmas, and Quinquagesima receives Dietrich's own collect [23]. TLHy followed the Dano-Norwegian pericopes, in which Quinquagesima (*Fastelavn*) carries the Baptism of Christ, Matthew 3:13–17; in the German pericopes Dietrich follows, Quinquagesima has Luke 18:31–43, and the Baptism falls on the Sunday after New Year. TLHy's own collect for the Second Sunday of Christmas is not Dietrich's (Appendix A, [11]).

**German (PDF pp. 42–43)**

> HErr Gott himlischer Vatter/der du dich mit deim heyligen Geyst/inn allen genaden bey der Tauffe deines lieben Suns/der vnser aller sünde auff sich geladen/hast offenbaret/vnnd vns mit deiner stim̃ zu jm gewisen/das wir durch jhn vergebung der sünde/vnd alle gnad haben sollen/Wir bitten dich/erhalt vns in solcher zuuersicht/vñ weil wir nach dem Befelh vnd Exempel deines Suns auch getauffet sind/Stercke vnsern glauben durch deinen heiligen Geist/vnd hilff vns zum ewigen lebē/AMEN.

**Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, who didst manifest Thyself, with Thy Holy Ghost, in all grace at the baptism of Thy dear Son, who hath taken upon Himself the sins of us all, and with Thy voice didst direct us to Him, that through Him we should have forgiveness of sins and all grace: Keep us, we beseech Thee, in this confidence; and inasmuch as we also have been baptized according to the command and example of Thy Son, we pray Thee to strengthen our faith by Thy Holy Ghost, and to help us unto eternal life; *through Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**
- "with the Holy Ghost" → "with Thy Holy Ghost" (*mit deim heyligen Geyst*).
- "in the fullness of grace" → "in all grace" (*inn allen genaden*).
- "and with Thy voice didst direct us to Him who hath borne our sins" → "who hath taken upon Himself the sins of us all, and with Thy voice didst direct us to Him" (*der vnser aller sünde auff sich geladen ... vnnd vns mit deiner stim̃ zu jm gewisen*). The clause is restored to its place and to its full wording (cf. Isaiah 53:6).
- "that we might receive grace and the remission of sins" → "that through Him we should have forgiveness of sins and all grace" (*das wir durch jhn vergebung der sünde/vnd alle gnad haben sollen*).
- "in the true faith" → "in this confidence" (*in solcher zuuersicht*).
- "inasmuch as we have been baptized in accordance with Thy command, and the example of Thy dear Son" → "inasmuch as we also have been baptized according to the command and example of Thy Son" (*weil wir nach dem Befelh vnd Exempel deines Suns auch getauffet sind*). Both the command and the example are the Son's.
- "and lead us to everlasting life and salvation" → "and to help us unto eternal life" (*vnd hilff vns zum ewigen lebē*).
- Dietrich gives no conclusion, so TLHy's ending is kept in italics. "Holy Ghost" for "Holy Spirit"; the OLM web text's "Amen," corrected.

### Epiphany

#### [12] The Epiphany of Our Lord

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 43):** *Am Oberstag/Wie die weysen auß dem morgenland gehn Hierusalem kom̃en/vnnd nach dem newgebornen kindlin fragen/Luc. 2.* [On Epiphany: how the wise men come from the East to Jerusalem and ask after the newborn child. Luke 2 [sic, for Matthew 2].]

**German (PDF pp. 50–51)**

> HErr Gott himlischer vatter/der du dein liebes wort den rechtē Stern/der das kindlin Jesus zeyget/vns hast lassen leuchten/wir bitten dich/gib deinen heiligen Geist in vnsere hertzen/das wir ja solches liecht annemen/vñ seligklich brauchen/das wir/wie die Weysen/solchem Stern nachziehen/kein mühe noch fahr vns lassen schrecken/sonder mit gantzē hertzen vns an deinen Sun Christum Jesum/als den eynigen Heyland halten/vnd vnnser zeitliches auch dazu wenden/das dein arme Christenheyt dadurch gebessert/vñ deinem Sun Christo Jesu gedienet werde/Amen.

**Revised TLHy**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, who hast caused Thy dear word, the true star which showeth the child Jesus, to shine upon us: We beseech Thee, give Thy Holy Ghost into our hearts, that we may indeed receive this light and make use of it unto our salvation; that we, like the wise men, may follow this star, and suffer no toil nor peril to affright us, but with our whole heart hold to Thy Son, Jesus Christ, as the only Savior, and turn our temporal goods also to this end, that Thy poor Christendom may thereby be bettered, and Thy Son, Jesus Christ, be served, *who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Changes from TLHy**
- "who hast given us the light of Thy holy word, the guiding star, which leadeth us to the Christ-child" → "who hast caused Thy dear word, the true star which showeth the child Jesus, to shine upon us" (*der du dein liebes wort den rechtē Stern/der das kindlin Jesus zeyget/vns hast lassen leuchten*).
- "Send, we beseech Thee, Thy Holy Spirit into our hearts" → "We beseech Thee, give Thy Holy Ghost into our hearts" (*gib deinen heiligen Geist in vnsere hertzen*; §2.2).
- "receive this light" → "indeed receive this light" (*ja*).
- "and that we, like the wise men, when they were seeking the star, may not be afraid because of any hardship or peril" → "that we, like the wise men, may follow this star, and suffer no toil nor peril to affright us" (*das wir/wie die Weysen/solchem Stern nachziehen/kein mühe noch fahr vns lassen schrecken*). - "but put all our trust in Thine only-begotten Son, Jesus Christ, our Lord, as our only Savior" → "but with our whole heart hold to Thy Son, Jesus Christ, as the only Savior" (*mit gantzē hertzen vns an deinen Sun Christum Jesum/als den eynigen Heyland halten*).
