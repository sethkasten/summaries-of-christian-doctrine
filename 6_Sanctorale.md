## PART II. SANCTORALE

### November and December

#### [79] St. Andrew, Apostle (November 30)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 230):** *An Sanct Andreas tag/Wie er zum Herren Christo komen/vnnd zum Apostelampt beruffen sey/Johannis am 1.* [On St. Andrew's Day: how he came to the Lord Christ and was called to the apostolic office. John 1.]

**German (PDF pp. 232–233)**

> HERR Gott himelischer Vatter/der du durch den heyligen Johannem am Jordan/deinen son Christum Jesum inn der welt hast offenbar gemacht/Wir bittē dich/du wöllest auch vnsere hertzen durch deinen heyligen geyst rüren/das wir mit dem heyligē Andrea/vns solcher predig von deynem Son hertzlich annemen/jhn für vnsern Seligmacher erkennen/vnd jm nachfolgen/vñ ja kein verfolgung vns von jhm lassen abweysen/biß wir durch jn gar selig werden/AMEN.

**New translation**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, who by Saint John at the Jordan didst make Thy Son, Jesus Christ, manifest in the world: We beseech Thee, move our hearts also by Thy Holy Ghost, that with Saint Andrew we may heartily embrace such preaching of Thy Son, acknowledge Him as our Savior, and follow Him, and by no means suffer any persecution to turn us away from Him, until we be wholly saved through Him, *who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Notes**
- **Rendering choices.** *rüren* means touch or move. *vns … annemen* (with genitive) means to embrace or take to heart. *gar selig* means wholly saved.
- **Ending.** Dietrich's closing "through Him" is moved to the end of its clause so that TLHy's "who liveth and reigneth…" can follow it; only the word order changes (§2.4c).

#### [80] St. Thomas, Apostle (December 21)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 233):** *An Sanct Thomas tag/Johannis am 20.* [On St. Thomas's Day. John 20.]

**German (PDF p. 236)**

> HErr Got himlischer Vatter/der du durch deynen Son vns armen sündern rath vnd hilf hast schaffen wöllen/Sey mir gnedig/vnnd hilffe/das ich des vnglaubens mich erwehren/vnd mit gantzem vertrawen auff dein wort begeben möge/Vnd O du Son Gottes/der du den vnglaubigen Thomas in solcher sünde nit stecken lassen/sonder jm gnedigklich geholffen hast/Gibe dein heyligen Geyst auch in mein hertz/das ich an deiner gnad nymmer mehr zweiffle/sonder durch vesten glauben auff deine barmhertzigkeit ewig selig werde/Amen.

**New translation**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, who through Thy Son hast been pleased to provide counsel and help for us poor sinners: Be gracious unto me, and help me, that I may resist unbelief, and with entire trust commit myself to Thy word. And O Thou Son of God, who didst not leave unbelieving Thomas stuck fast in such sin, but didst graciously help him: Give Thy Holy Ghost into my heart also, that I may nevermore doubt Thy grace, but through firm faith in Thy mercy obtain eternal salvation, *Thou who livest and reignest with the Father and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Congregational form (TLHy practice)**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, who through Thy Son hast been pleased to provide counsel and help for us poor sinners: Be gracious unto us, and help us, that we may resist unbelief, and with entire trust commit ourselves to Thy word. And O Thou Son of God, who didst not leave unbelieving Thomas stuck fast in such sin, but didst graciously help him: Give Thy Holy Ghost into our hearts also, that we may nevermore doubt Thy grace, but through firm faith in Thy mercy obtain eternal salvation, *Thou who livest and reignest with the Father and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Notes**
- **Two addresses.** The prayer is in the first person singular and is addressed first to the Father and then to the Son. Because its last petition is to the Son, it takes TLHy's ending for a prayer to Christ (§2.4e).
- **Rendering choices.** *rath vnd hilf* means counsel (remedy) and help. *sich … erwehren* (with genitive) means to fend off or resist. *sich … begeben auff* means to commit oneself to.

#### [81] St. Stephen, Martyr (December 26)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 30):** *An Sanct Steffans tag/in den geschichten/wie er vmb des Herrn Christi willen/von Juden ist gesteyniget worden/Acto. 7.* [On St. Stephen's Day, from the Acts: how he was stoned by the Jews for the sake of the Lord Christ. Acts 7.] In the 1548 book this section stands in the Christmas sequence, between Christmas and the Sunday after Christmas.

**Assignment:** St. Stephen's Day, beside TLHy's present collect, which is not Dietrich's ("O eternal and merciful God, our heavenly Father, who didst give such grace to Saint Stephen…"; Appendix A, [82]).

**German (PDF pp. 32–33)**

> HErr Gott himlischer Vatter/der du den heiligen Steffanum seins glaubens halb/leyden vñ steynigen hast lassen/vns damit anzuzeigen das dein reich nit von diser welt sey/sonder in ein anders leben gehöre/Wir bitē dich/wo wir vmb deines worts vñ namens willē auch müssen leidē/sterck vnser hertzē durch deinen heiligē geist/das wir fest bestehn/vñ vns mit deiner gnad/vnd den ewigen gütern/anstat der zeitlichen trösten mögen/Amen.

**New translation**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, who didst permit Saint Stephen to suffer and to be stoned for his faith's sake, thereby to show us that Thy kingdom is not of this world, but belongeth to another life: We beseech Thee, if we also must suffer for the sake of Thy word and name, strengthen our hearts by Thy Holy Ghost, that we may stand fast, and comfort ourselves with Thy grace and with the eternal goods in place of the temporal; *through Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Notes**
- **God's purpose.** *vns damit anzuzeigen* states what God intended, "thereby to show us." It is not something Stephen showed.

#### [83] The Holy Innocents (December 28)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 237):** *An der vnschuldigen kindlein tag/Matthei am 2.* [On the Day of the Innocent Children. Matthew 2.]

**German (PDF pp. 239–240)**

> HErr Got himlischer Vatter/der du deinen Son Christum Jhesum/durch dein wort vns offenbaret hast/du sihest/wie noch heutiges tages der Teuffel seine Herodes hat/vnd wider das wort reytzet vnd treibet/mit allerley verfolgung/Wir bitten dich/du wöllest den schedlichen Tyrannen wehren/alles vnglück/das sie vber deine Christliche Kirch gedencken/auff jren kopf schütten/vnd vns frid vnd ruhe genedigklich schaffen/auff das dein wort ye lenger ye weytter außbreytet/vñ wir deinen gnedigen schutz vnd hilff von tag zu tag/ye lenger ye mehr erkennen vnnd preysen mögen/AMEN.

**New translation**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, who hast revealed unto us Thy Son, Jesus Christ, through Thy word: Thou seest how even unto this day the devil hath his Herods, and inciteth and driveth on against the word with all manner of persecution. We beseech Thee, restrain the harmful tyrants; pour out upon their own heads all the mischief which they devise against Thy Christian Church; and graciously grant us peace and quiet, that Thy word may spread abroad ever farther and farther, and that from day to day we may ever more and more know and praise Thy gracious protection and help; *through the same, Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Notes**
- **Rendering choices.** *reytzet vnd treibet* has no object expressed in the German, so the English leaves none. *auff jren kopf schütten* echoes Psalm 7:16. *ye lenger ye weytter/ye mehr* means "ever farther / ever more."

#### [86] St. Matthias, Apostle (February 24)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 247):** *An Sanct Matthias tag/Matthei am 9.* [On St. Matthias's Day. Matthew 9 [sic].] The text expounded (PDF p. 248, "Kombt her zu mir/alle die jr mühselig vnd beladen seydt…") is Matthew 11:25–30.

**German (PDF pp. 250–251)**

> HErr Got himlischer vater Wir dancken dir für deine vnaußsprechliche gnade/dz du vns arme sünder bedacht/vñ deinen Sun in vnser fleisch geschickt/vnd vmb vnsert willen hast lassen mensch werden. Vñ bitten dich du w[ö]llest [type damaged] durch deinen heyligen geist vnsere hertzen also erleuchtē/das wir vns seiner menschwerdung/leydens vnd sterbens trösten/jhn für vnsern herrn vnd ewigen König erkennen/vnd annemen/vnnd durch jn mit dir vnnd deinem heyligen Geyst/ewig leben/vnd selig werden/Amen/Amen.

**New translation**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, we thank Thee for Thine ineffable grace, that Thou hast been mindful of us poor sinners, and hast sent Thy Son into our flesh, and for our sakes hast suffered Him to become man; and we beseech Thee so to enlighten our hearts by Thy Holy Ghost, that we may comfort ourselves with His incarnation, suffering, and death, acknowledge and receive Him as our Lord and everlasting King, and through Him live eternally with Thee and Thy Holy Ghost, and be saved. Amen, Amen.

**Notes**
- **A duplicated prayer.** In this edition the Matthias prayer is the Annunciation prayer [87], differing only in spelling and in the doubled "Amen/Amen." It does not reflect the Matthias exposition, which treats the easy yoke and light burden of Matthew 11:28–30. The section's heading also misprints the chapter.
- **Likely cause.** Together these point to a printer's duplication. Comparing another edition of the *Summaria* would settle whether Dietrich wrote a distinct Matthias prayer.
- **Ending.** No TLHy ending is added (§2.4g).

#### [92] The Visitation (July 2)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 272):** *Am tag der heymsuchung Marie/Luc. am 1.* [On the Day of the Visitation of Mary. Luke 1.]

**German (PDF pp. 275–276)**

> HErr Got himlischer Vatter/Wir dancken dir für alle deine wolthat/leiblich vnnd geystlich/die du so reychlich [catchword "reichlich"] vns hast widerfaren lassen/Vnd bitten dich/behüte für hoffart/vnd sicherheyt/das wir nicht in vndanck vnd sünd gerathen/vnnd dein huld verlieren/wie die liebe Junckfraw Maria drowet/dz weder weißheyt/gewalt noch gelt die helffen sol/die dich nicht förchten/Gib aber vns ein solches hertz/das für vnnd für in deiner forcht bleybe/vnnd an deinem wort hange/auf das dein segen bey vns bleybe/vnd wir durch dein gnad ewig selig werden/AMEN.

**New translation**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, we thank Thee for all Thy benefits, bodily and spiritual, which Thou hast so richly bestowed upon us; and we beseech Thee, keep us from pride and carnal security, that we fall not into ingratitude and sin, and lose Thy favor; as the dear Virgin Mary threateneth, that neither wisdom, power, nor money shall help them that fear Thee not. But give us such a heart as may abide evermore in Thy fear, and cleave unto Thy word, that Thy blessing may remain with us, and we through Thy grace obtain eternal salvation; *through Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Notes**
- **Supplied object.** *behüte* has no object in the German; "us" is supplied, as TLHy does with the same construction at Epiphany 5.
- **"Threateneth."** *drowet* (*dräuet*) means "threatens." It reads the Magnificat (Luke 1:51–53) as a threat against the proud, the mighty and the rich, here wisdom, power and money.

#### [94] St. James the Elder, Apostle (July 25)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 276):** *An Sanct Jacobs tage/Marci am 10.* [On St. James's Day. Mark 10.]

**German (PDF p. 280)**

> HErr Got himlischer Vatter/Es thut vns das creutze sehr wehe/vnd wolten sein gern vberhoben sein. ### August to October

#### [95] St. Bartholomew, Apostle (August 24)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 281):** *An Sanct Bartholomeus tag/Luce am 22.* [On St. Bartholomew's Day. Luke 22.]

**German (PDF p. 284)**

> HErr Got himlischer Vatter/du hast dein kirch mit trewen Dienern besetzet/das sie dein wort füren/vnd yederman durch jren dienst dahin sollen bringē/das sie dich erkennen/in deiner forcht wandlen/vñ durch Christum selig werden/Wir bitten dich/du wöllest mit deinem heiligen Geyst alle solche Kirchendiener regieren/das sie jres diensts trewlich warten/Vnd durch geytz/hoffart/oder anders/vom Sathan nicht bethöret/noch in jhrem dienst laß vnnd faul werden/auff das dein reych gemehret/vnnd des Sathans zerstöret werde/Amen.

**New translation**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, Thou hast furnished Thy Church with faithful servants, who should bear Thy word, and by their ministry bring every man to know Thee, to walk in Thy fear, and through Christ to be saved: We beseech Thee, govern all such servants of the Church by Thy Holy Ghost, that they may faithfully wait on their ministry, and be neither beguiled by Satan through covetousness, pride, or aught else, nor grow slack and slothful in their ministry; that Thy kingdom may be increased, and that of Satan destroyed; *through the same, Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Notes**
- **Rendering choices.**
  - *Dienern … Kirchendiener* are both given as "servants," to keep Dietrich's link. TLHy renders *Kirchendiener* as "pastors" at the Ascension.
  - *trewlich warten* is "faithfully wait on" (cf. Romans 12:7, King James).
  - *bethöret* means befooled or beguiled.
- **Date.** Stratman dates this feast August 25; the traditional date is August 24.

#### [96] St. Matthew, Apostle and Evangelist (September 21)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 285):** *An Sanct Mattheus tag/Matthei am 9.* [On St. Matthew's Day. Matthew 9.]

**German (PDF p. 288)**

> HErr Got himlischer Vatter/du hast vnns durch dein liebes wort/zu deynem Reych beruffen/da vergebung der sünden/vñ alle genad vnd barmhertzigkeyt jnne ist/Jch bitte dich/du wöllest durch deinen heyligen Geyst/vnsere hertzen erwecken/vnnd erleuchten/das wir/wie Mattheus der Zolner/solchen beruff annemen/von sünden ablassen/vnd vns bessern/vnnd aller gnad durch deinen Son Christum Jesum/vns zu dir versehen/vnd also endlich selig werden/AMEN.

**New translation**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, Thou hast called us by Thy dear word into Thy kingdom, wherein is forgiveness of sins and all grace and mercy: I beseech Thee, quicken and enlighten our hearts by Thy Holy Ghost, that we, like Matthew the publican, may accept such a call, forsake sin, and amend our lives, and look to Thee for all grace through Thy Son, Jesus Christ, and so at last be saved; *through the same, Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Congregational form (TLHy practice):** Read "We beseech Thee" for "I beseech Thee"; the rest is unchanged.

**Notes**
- **Mixed number.** The German mixes "Jch bitte dich" with "vnsere hertzen … das wir"; it is kept as printed.
- **Rendering choices.** *erwecken* is TLHy's "quicken" (Trinity 3). *aller gnad … vns zu dir versehen* means "to look to Thee for all grace" (*sich zu jemandem einer Sache versehen*, to expect something confidently from someone).

#### [97] St. Michael and All Angels (September 29)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 289):** *An Sanct Michaels tag/In dem 91. Psalm.* [On St. Michael's Day. In the 91st Psalm.]

**German (PDF p. 292).** There is no "Gebet" rubric: the prayer follows directly after the catchword "Herr" on p. 291.

> HERR Gott himlischer Vatter/Wir danckē dir für deine Vätterliche barmhertzigkeyt/das du deine liebe Engel dazu verordnet hast/das sie auff vns sehen/vnnd wider des leydigen Sathans fürnemen vns schützen sollen/Vnd bitten dich/regier vnnd füre vnsere hertzen vnnd sinn/durch deinen heiligen Geyst dermassen/das wir in deiner forcht vns halten/vnnd wider deinen willen nicht thun/vnnd also in allerley zufallenden nöten/die zuuersicht mögen behalten/das deine liebe Engel werden vmb vns/vnd alles was wir haben/einen starcken vesten zaun machen/das weder der Sathan/noch die arge welt einbrechen/vnnd schaden möge thun/auff das gleich wie durch deinen lieben Sun Christum Jhesum/vns vnsere sünde vergeben/vnnd das ewige leben versprochē ist/also durch den schutz deiner lieben Engel/leyb/leben/vñ alles was wir auff erden haben/möge erhalten vnd befridet werden. Amen.

**New translation**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, we thank Thee for Thy fatherly mercy, that Thou hast ordained Thy dear angels to watch over us and to defend us against the designs of the hateful Satan; and we beseech Thee, so rule and direct our hearts and minds by Thy Holy Ghost, that we may keep ourselves in Thy fear and do nothing against Thy will, and so in all manner of need that befalleth us may retain the confidence that Thy dear angels will make a strong and sure hedge about us and all that we have, so that neither Satan nor the wicked world may break in and do harm; that, even as through Thy dear Son, Jesus Christ, our sins are forgiven us and eternal life is promised us, so through the protection of Thy dear angels body, life, and all that we have on earth may be preserved and protected; *through the same, Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Notes**
- **Rendering choices.**
  - *leydig* means hateful or accursed.
  - *zaun* is a "hedge" (cf. Job 1:10, King James: "made an hedge about him … and about all that he hath").
  - *befridet* means protected; literally "kept in peace," or "fenced about."

#### [98] Sts. Simon and Jude, Apostles (October 28)

**Dietrich's heading (PDF p. 293):** *Am tag Simonis vnd Jude/Johan. am 15.* [On the Day of Simon and Jude. John 15.]

**German (PDF p. 296)**

> HERR Gott hymlischer Vater/der du dich durch deinen Sun Christum Jesum hast offenbaret/Wir bitten dich/gibe deinen heyligen Geyst inn sere [sic, for vnsere] hertzen/das er die rechte Christliche liebe inn vns anzünde/vnnd vor allem dem behütte vnnd beware/das der liebe entgegen ist/Vnnd daneben vns auch tröste vnnd stercke inn aller widerwertigkeyt/so deines worts halb vns begegnet/das wir vns an dein gnad vnd wort vest halten/vnd der welt vnd des Teuffels zorn verachten mögen/Amen.

**New translation**

> Lord God, heavenly Father, who hast revealed Thyself through Thy Son, Jesus Christ: We beseech Thee, give Thy Holy Ghost into our hearts, that He may kindle in us true Christian love, and guard and keep us from all that is contrary to love; and that He may moreover comfort and strengthen us in all adversity which befalleth us for the sake of Thy word, that we may hold fast to Thy grace and word, and despise the wrath of the world and of the devil; *through the same, Thy beloved Son, Jesus Christ our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with Thee and the Holy Ghost, one true God, world without end.* Amen.

**Notes**
- **Supplied object.** *behütte vnnd beware* has no object in the German; "us" is supplied.
